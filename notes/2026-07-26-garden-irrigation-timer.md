# Irrigation timer schedule

Set the timer to two zones.

- Zone 1 (beds): 6:00 AM, 12 minutes, daily in July and August, every other day in September.
- Zone 2 (lawn): 5:30 AM, 20 minutes, Mon/Wed/Fri.

Replaced two clogged drip emitters. The tomatoes were wilting by afternoon before the change; fine now.
The timer is on the IoT VLAN and phones home constantly; Pi-hole shows about 400 queries a day from it.
