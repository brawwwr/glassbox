# Sourdough attempt 3

Third loaf. Better. Changes from attempt 2: 78% hydration instead of 82%, bulk ferment 5 hours at 76 F,
cold retard overnight, baked at 475 F in the dutch oven, 20 minutes lid on, 25 lid off.

Crumb is open but not gummy. Ear formed. Bottom slightly too dark; next time put a baking sheet on the
rack below.
