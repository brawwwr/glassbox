# Backup plan for the trip

If the first campsite is full, the backup plan is the state park twenty minutes north. Reservations are
not required there on weekdays. Bring cash for the day-use fee. If both are full, the motel in town has
a pool and takes dogs.

Pack list is in the shared note with M. Do not forget the coffee grinder again.
