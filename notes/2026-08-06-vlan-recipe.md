# VLAN: the sandwich

Named it the VLAN because everything is separated into layers and nothing touches. Veal, lettuce,
aioli, naan. Toast the naan first. The aioli is just garlic, lemon, egg yolk and oil; do not rush the oil.

It is a good sandwich. It has nothing to do with networking.
