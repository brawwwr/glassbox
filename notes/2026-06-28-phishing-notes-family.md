# Phishing talk for the family, notes

Gave the twenty-minute version at dinner. What landed:

- Urgency is the tell. "Your account will be closed in 24 hours" is the pattern.
- Hover, do not click. On the phone, long-press to see the link.
- Banks do not ask you to move money to a "safe account".
- If in doubt, call the real number from the card, not the number in the message.

Set up the parents' phones with the carrier's spam filter and a shared note of "who to call".
