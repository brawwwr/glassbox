# VLAN plan for the home network

Decided on four VLANs. Keeping it simple so I actually finish it.

| VLAN | Name    | Subnet          | Purpose                          |
|------|---------|-----------------|----------------------------------|
| 10   | trusted | 192.168.10.0/24 | laptops, desktop, NAS            |
| 20   | iot     | 192.168.20.0/24 | thermostat, cameras, smart plugs |
| 30   | guest   | 192.168.30.0/24 | visitors; internet only          |
| 40   | lab     | 192.168.40.0/24 | VMs, test boxes, anything I might break |

Rules: iot and guest cannot initiate to trusted. Lab can reach trusted on 445 (SMB) only.
Pi-hole on trusted serves DNS to everything. Cameras get NTP and nothing else outbound.

Open question: does the thermostat need mDNS across VLANs? Probably yes; test before locking down.
