# UPS runtime test

Pulled the plug on the UPS with the NAS, switch, firewall and Pi on it. Desktop was not on the UPS.

Runtime to 20% battery: 34 minutes. NAS shut down cleanly via the USB signal at 15%.
That is enough to ride out the short outages we get, and to shut down gracefully for longer ones.

Battery is from 2022; plan to replace in 2027. Added a calendar reminder.
