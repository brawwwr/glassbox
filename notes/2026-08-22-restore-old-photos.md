# Restoring the old photos

Grandmother's photos from the 1950s, faded and curled. Flattened them under books for a week, scanned at
600 dpi, then did light colour correction. Did not try to remove the scratches; they are part of it.

Printed three for the wall. The scans are in the photos folder under 1950s.
